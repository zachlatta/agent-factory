Can I trust these numbers?

Waiting for coverage…

Source coverage and counting rules

Waiting for a bounded scan. This is not complete history.

Missing or unsupported records are unavailable, not evidence of zero usage. Unknown token volume cannot be estimated from a session count. Errors can leave known counts partial.

Historical account attribution is unknown. Current account allowances do not identify which account paid for earlier tokens. These figures cannot be reconciled as a bill.

Initial Codex cumulative readings and intervals crossing UTC days are excluded from dated summaries. Unknown dates remain in All retained history. Intervals crossing stage boundaries, conflicting owners, and missing or expired timing stay unassigned. Timing history is retained for 30 days. A recorded visit (span) identifies repeated Building visits; a review round identifies repeated review. Built-in sub-agent files use their owner's card and stage.

Each scan is bounded to 2,000 changed metadata files, 32 MiB per session per refresh, 128 MiB overall and 20 seconds. LIMITED means counts are partial. Another bounded slice can start after 15 seconds while the page is open. Complete snapshots are cached for five minutes. Reloading does not force a collection.

Cleanup can remove records and shrink totals or alter comparisons. Disappeared sources are detected only against this process's prior inventory on complete scans. Restarting forgets that inventory. Comparisons describe the records retained now, not an immutable ledger.
